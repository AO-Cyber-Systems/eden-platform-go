<this_is_an_auth_surface>
A preview token is a bearer credential for unpublished content. Treat it as such:
generated from crypto/rand, scoped to a single page and tenant, with a hard expiry.
A token that never expires, or that is guessable, is a content leak.

Equally, scheduled publishing is a correctness trap: store an absolute UTC instant.
A wall-clock time without a zone publishes early or late, and "early" is a leak.
</this_is_an_auth_surface>
