<the_seam>
platform/cms emits `[]Block`. A renderer maps `Block.Type` to a component and renders
`Block.Data`. eden-web is the recommended renderer and already has a `stories` Registry
keyed by component name.

The dependency arrow must NOT be drawn: eden-web lives in the eden-libs repo as its own
Go module, and eden-platform-go releases independently. Importing it here would couple
two repos' release cycles. The consumer wires the two together.
</the_seam>
