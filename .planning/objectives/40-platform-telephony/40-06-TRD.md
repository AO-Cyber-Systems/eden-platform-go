<the_order_is_the_security_property>
capture raw body → verify signature → resolve tenant by receiving number → parse → act.

Parsing before verifying means acting on attacker-controlled input. Resolving the
tenant from a body field rather than the receiving number means an attacker picks
the tenant. Both must be impossible by construction, and both must have a test.
</the_order_is_the_security_property>

<constraints>
- The handler MUST buffer the raw body before any parse and pass those exact bytes to
  `VerifyWebhookSignature`.
- Reject with a non-2xx on signature failure. Do not log the body at a level that would
  persist unverified attacker input.
- Compare justinforme's inbound/status handlers; if they cover a status transition
  politihub misses, add it with a test.
</constraints>
